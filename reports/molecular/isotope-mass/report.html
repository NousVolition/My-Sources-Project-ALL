A heavier molecule
in a group of nine

A reproducible mass-only isotope test with explicit water interactions, randomized ensembles and intervention-based response measurements.

What this test establishes

Across all nine placements, the mean H-bond degree changed by 0.0029383 (0.18347%; 95% interval -0.0061886 to 0.012852). Nearest-neighbor exchange changed by -0.00028164 per ps (-0.0025246%; interval -0.043962 to 0.045787). Interpret these model estimates with the sensitivity tables below. The declared 0.5 ps response endpoint failed numerical checks.

There is a measurable short-time inertial response in the refined matched-state assay, concentrated in interactions involving the substituted molecule. This does not establish collective reorganization or a persistent leader. All findings concern confined, classical TIP3P water. They do not establish an isotope effect in real liquid water.

What actually ran

544 full trajectories completed: 32 all-light and 288 single-heavy main trajectories, 32 repeated label controls, and 192 sensitivity trajectories. The main ensemble has 16 independent randomized configurations, each with two velocity/thermostat seeds. Repeated frames, molecules, isotope placements and shared-start velocity seeds are not counted as independent initial configurations.

The model and its boundary

Each molecule has one oxygen and two hydrogen sites, rigid TIP3P geometry, oxygen Lennard-Jones interactions and Coulomb interactions between partial charges. No interaction cutoff or periodic replicas are used. A flat-bottom spherical wall acts on oxygens: Uwall = (10000/2) max(0, r − R)² kJ/mol, with r and R in nm. R = 0.4 nm for nine molecules; the 27-molecule radius is 0.4 × 31/3 nm. A separate test uses R = 0.5 nm.

The wall prevents indefinite dispersal of a tiny warm cluster. It supplies external forces and breaks translational momentum conservation. This is a model of confined water with strong finite-size and surface effects; it is not a nine-particle sample of bulk liquid. Whole-group membership is fixed. “Residence time” below means time inside another oxygen’s neighbor shell, not residence in the entire group.

The engine is OpenMM 8.6.1 Reference (double precision). All simulations use 300 K LangevinMiddle temperature control, independent seed blocks, minimization, equilibration and rigid constraints at 10−8. The main time step is 1 fs and friction is 1 ps−1. The thermostat affects dynamical rates; weaker-friction and half-step tests are included. Production coordinates and energies are saved every 0.05 ps. Details, seeds and exact units are in protocol.json and the raw metadata.

The oxygen mass is 15.99943 Da; hydrogen is 1.007947 Da and deuterium 2.01410177812 Da. Only the two hydrogen masses of the selected molecule change. The mass increase for the whole D₂O molecule is about 11.2%, so this is not an infinitesimal perturbation. Charges, Lennard-Jones parameters, bond lengths, angles and wall forces are identical. Atom labels remain water labels; physical masses define the isotope model.

Organization and dynamics

A hydrogen-bond edge exists when O–O is below 0.35 nm and a donor O–H···O angle is at least 150°. Either donating direction produces one undirected edge. This geometric edge is not a covalent bond. Oxygen coordination uses the same distance without the angular test. Degree is the number of incident edges; structural centrality is the normalized principal eigenvector of the trajectory-average adjacency matrix.

Nearest-neighbor exchange counts changes of the nearest oxygen at consecutive saved frames, including recrossings. H-bond and shell-residence lifetimes use newly observed episodes, excluding episodes already active at the start and retaining right censoring at the end. Kaplan–Meier restricted mean lifetime integrates survival to 2 ps. They are distinct from the probability that an occupied bond survives without interruption for 0.5 ps. Reformations count as new episodes. These definitions follow the need to separate continuous and intermittent bond dynamics discussed by Luzar and Chandler (1996).

Remote network structure uses only edges between untagged molecules. Its main mass effect is 0.0027439 [-0.0057246, 0.011932] degree units. A group mean can change solely because the tagged molecule changes; that alone is not evidence for a collective hierarchy. The same all-light trajectory with randomly reassigned identities gives a maximum coordinate mismatch of 0 nm after undoing the permutation, across 32 controls. A separate reordered-particle NVE test checks physical permutation symmetry. Identities have no independent causal force in this model.

A causal response assay, with a failed long-lag check

At three production snapshots, each source receives equal and opposite total momentum kicks of magnitude 0.05 Da nm/ps along each Cartesian axis. All atoms of the source receive the same added velocity, preserving rigid constraints. Paired branches evolve without thermostat for lags 0, 0.02, 0.1 and 0.5 ps. Rij is the root mean square over axes of the central difference in target oxygen position divided by twice the impulse. Units are ps/Da. Off-diagonal R measures transmitted response; direct self-motion is excluded. Kicks have equal momentum, not equal energy. The measure is conditional on the state, intervention and observation time.

The original 0.5 ps endpoint is not numerically reliable. Halving the original step or kick changed some response matrices substantially. A further test at 0.25 and 0.125 fs and impulses 0.0125 and 0.00625 Da nm/ps still leaves the 0.5 ps endpoint sensitive. The response means and ranks at that lag are saved for audit, not accepted as evidence of influence ordering. Maximum unperturbed 0.5 ps energy range over the production snapshots was 0.11721 kJ/mol; energy stability alone does not guarantee response convergence.

Persistence and sensitivity

For all-light main trajectories, the molecule with the highest mean degree in the first half also has the highest mean degree in the second half in 18.75% of trajectories. This is descriptive, not a test against a 1/9 null: time windows are correlated and labels began in different configurations. Degree-rank correlations are shown below. Finite-window centrality or response differences do not demonstrate a persistent leader; a reliable leader claim would require sustained, independently validated intervention effects across times, permutations, sites and model choices.

What remains untested

  • Bulk water with periodic PME and validated system-size, concentration and long-time convergence; a constant-fraction comparison with three heavy molecules among 27.
  • Independent force fields, a wall-free low-temperature cluster ensemble, sub-50-fs contact sampling and substantially more independent configurations.
  • Nuclear quantum effects, flexible anharmonic O–H/O–D vibrations, polarizability, isotope exchange chemistry and real experimental validation.
  • A converged long-lag causal-response network, a persistent leader, and any extrapolation to Navier–Stokes physics or evolutionary change in H₂O.

At fixed interaction potential, the classical equilibrium positional density is proportional to exp(−U/kT); integrating momenta yields mass factors that cancel upon normalization. Independent rigid molecules likewise contribute constant mass/inertia factors. Thus a reproducible structural isotope effect at classical equilibrium would require scrutiny of equilibration, sampling, constraints or a changed potential. Quantum nuclei need not obey this mass independence. Recent path-integral water research finds isotope effects especially important for dynamics. This calculation includes classical inertia but omits those quantum effects.

Reproduce and inspect

6 implementation tests passed: isotope force invariance, hydrogen-bond geometry, episode censoring, light and heavy molecular-identity permutations, cluster inference and absence of transmitted response when intermolecular forces are removed (several checks share one test). These checks are separate from the failed scientific long-lag convergence check. Full artifact verification also checks raw inventories, rigid geometry, saved data and relabeled network/response statistics.

Use Python 3.12 with the pinned dependencies. Run python test_science.py, python simulate.py --workers 4, python switch_assay.py, python refine_response.py, python analyze.py, python make_report.py, and python verify_results.py, in that order. Windows users can run reproduce.ps1. Existing trajectory records are resumed; use an empty data directory for a fresh simulation. Same-platform seeded Reference runs are reproducible, but exact bitwise agreement across software/platforms is not promised.

The archive includes scripts, exact protocol, 544 raw trajectory archives, response branches, XML systems, seeds, trajectory/molecule CSVs, bootstrap cluster values, machine-readable results, numerical failures and SHA-256 provenance. The repository tracks scripts and compact results; the larger raw NPZ files remain in the local research directory and the delivered archive.
