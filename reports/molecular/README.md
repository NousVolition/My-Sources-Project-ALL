# Molecular studies

- [One heavy water molecule: isotope-mass test](isotope-mass/README.md): completed confined classical TIP3P simulations with mass-only substitutions, all nine initial placements, identity controls, network/lifetime statistics and lagged interventions. The long-lag response check failed and is reported as unreliable.

These studies use explicit molecular interactions. The project's passive fluid tracers have no molecular masses or interaction forces and are not interchangeable with these models.
