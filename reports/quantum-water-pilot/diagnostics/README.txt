These are actual early diagnostic outputs, retained to expose failures.

initial-vibrations contains the original runs before independent isotope mass
weighting and tighter geometry optimization. Its frequency_cm1 fields are the
untrusted built-in xTB values, including the incorrect identical H/D spectra.
They are not the reported predictions and must not be pooled with final data.

initial-parser-failure retains raw output from an earlier parsing attempt.

Only vibration-final is used for the published frequency tables. The corrections
and unchanged acceptance thresholds are documented in ../amendment.json.
